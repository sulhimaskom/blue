## 🚀 Partner Enablement & Training

### Certification Programs

#### **Technical Certification Path**

**Level 1: Foundation Technician**
**Duration**: 2 weeks  
**Prerequisites**: Basic software development knowledge  
**Modules**:

- Platform architecture and principles
- Basic application generation and deployment
- Integration with common systems
- Troubleshooting and support basics
- Customer success fundamentals

**Assessment**: Practical exam with real-world scenarios  
**Certification**: Architect Platform Certified Technician (APCT)

**Level 2: Solutions Architect**
**Duration**: 4 weeks  
**Prerequisites**: Foundation Technician certification  
**Modules**:

- Advanced platform configuration and optimization
- Enterprise architecture and integration patterns
- Industry-specific solution design
- Performance optimization and scaling
- Security and compliance implementation
- Project management and delivery

**Assessment**: Complex solution design project + written exam  
**Certification**: Architect Platform Certified Architect (APCA)

**Level 3: Enterprise Consultant**
**Duration**: 6 weeks  
**Prerequisites**: Solutions Architect certification  
**Modules**:

- Strategic consulting and business transformation
- Large-scale enterprise deployments
- Change management and organizational adoption
- Advanced customization and solution development
- Partner leadership and team management
- Innovation and thought leadership

**Assessment**: Real enterprise transformation project  
**Certification**: Architect Platform Certified Consultant (APCC)
